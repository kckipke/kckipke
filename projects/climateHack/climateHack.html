ClimateHack.AI satellite nowcasting

Python
PyTorch
WAE-GAN
Deep learning

This project was my entry for the ClimateHack.AI competition: predict the next two hours of UK Met Office rain-radar satellite imagery from twelve past 128×128 frames. I explored generative deep learning approaches to the nowcasting problem.

Features:

  • WAE-GAN architecture with convolutional encoder, transposed-convolution decoder, and MLP discriminator.
  • Joint reconstruction (MSE) and adversarial training with separate optimizers and learning-rate schedulers.
  • Custom dataset loader for preprocessed climate satellite data (xarray, 5-minute intervals).
  • Also explored DCGAN and convolutional autoencoder baselines for comparison.
  • MS-SSIM loss for perceptual image similarity evaluation.
  • DOXA submission pipeline targeting 24×64×64 output frames from 12×128×128 input sequences.
